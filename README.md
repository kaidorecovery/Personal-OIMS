# Personal iOMS

Your work, money and day in one app. It runs in the browser and installs on iPhone and Android from a link.

- **Your data stays on your phone.** Nothing is sent anywhere, except the text or photo you ask the AI to read.
- **AI reading (optional).** *Log anything* and the weekly suggestion use your own Anthropic API key. The app asks for it the first time you tap *Read it*, and keeps it on your phone only.
- **Works offline** once it has been opened.
- **Back up** under *Backup* now and then: there is no account, so the backup file is your copy.

## Install on a phone
- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, then tap Install app (or the three-dot menu, then Add to Home screen).

## First steps
1. Open **Your details** and add your name, company, address and bank details. They go on your invoices.
2. Set your rotation in the **Planner**.
3. Log your first day from **Home**.
